Stride Relayer Map

Who has to relay what, in which phase of the wind-down. Light-client state was read live on 23–24 Sep 2026; every chain holding any stToken is shown, and the decision per chain comes from scripts/wind-down/build_relayer_scope.py (the "Relayer scope per chain" table in sttoken-locations.md), which also regenerates this page's data. Line width follows the USD of stTokens on the chain (log scale). Chains marked with a filled centre are host zones whose native tokens also travel that spoke.

"Last header" is how long ago the verifying chain's light client last accepted a header from the other chain; relayers update it whenever they deliver a packet, so a fresh client means someone is relaying that direction. In phase 1 the phase-1 spoke checks both directions; in phases 2 and 3 only Osmosis's client is checked, because that is what verifies packets into Osmosis. Left out on purpose: the stTokens of the deprecated zones (stEVMOS, stDYM, stSTARS, stCMDX, stUMEE), the stopped chains (Evmos, Stargaze, Comdex, Umee, Kujira), stSOMM with Sommelier (too small), and every location holding under $1k of a token. Dymension appears only as a holder of stTIA.
