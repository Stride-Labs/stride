"Last header" is how long ago the verifying chain's light client last accepted a header from the other chain; relayers update it whenever they deliver a packet, so a fresh client means someone is relaying that direction. In phase 1 the phase-1 spoke checks both directions; in phases 2 and 3 only Osmosis's client is checked, because that is what verifies packets into Osmosis. The five deprecated zones (Evmos, Dymension, Stargaze, Comdex, Umee) and their stTokens, and Kujira (chain appears stopped), are left out on purpose: they are not part of the migration.
